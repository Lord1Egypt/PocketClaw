PocketClaw mark — optical refinement

The vc38 review found the mark reads slightly like a crown or a fork at small sizes rather than unmistakably pocket + claw. This page is the evidence for what changed and what did not. The concept is not under review here — only its optics.

What the small sizes actually showed

Rendered at 16px, the original geometry puts four verticals of equal weight across the middle band: the two pocket walls at x=4 and x=20, and the two claw arms at x=8.5 and x=15.5. The container's walls are only eight units tall and end in bare stroke caps, so nothing tells the eye which pair is the container. Four evenly spaced prongs is a fork. That is the whole defect, and it is geometric rather than a matter of taste.

Measured, not judged

Each variant was rasterised at 16× supersampling and read back. Two numbers decide it: how many separate inked runs cross the middle band (four equal ones is the fork), and how wide the unbroken floor run is relative to the icon (a container needs one).

What shipped, and what did not

Only refinement A is in vc39, because it is the one change that is an optical correction rather than a new brand decision: same concept, same 24-unit grid, same 2-unit stroke, same three paths. The web favicons and PWA icons are re-rendered from it by scripts/generate-brand-assets.py. The Android launcher icon is untouched in this phase. B and C are here for you to look at and are not wired to anything.
